A Visual Analysis of Weekday–Weekend Hotspots and Flows in NYC Bike Share

Team 17 · Xi Qiao (xq816), Shiyu Liu (sl13528) · All visualizations are built with D3 v7.

Research question

How does Citi Bike use in New York City differ between weekdays and weekends: where rides are concentrated (hotspots), and how bikes move between stations (flows)?

Our working hypothesis is that weekday riding is mostly commuting, with sharp peaks, predictable directions and more activity around business districts, while weekend riding is more leisure-oriented, spread through the day, centered on parks and waterfronts, and has a larger share of casual riders. The answer matters for operations: if demand moves to different places on weekends, bike rebalancing and station planning need to take the day of the week into account.

Scope

  • Trips that started between 1 and 30 September 2026 (Citi Bike System Data).
  • Only trips that start and end at stations inside the Manhattan borough boundary (NYC Open Data).
  • Labor Day (7 Sept) is excluded, which leaves 21 ordinary weekdays and 8 weekend days. Averages are per day of each type.
  • Trips need a unique ride ID, a positive duration and valid station IDs and coordinates at both ends. Station IDs that differ only by a trailing zero (same name, within 2 m) are combined for the maps.
  • Data and processing: manhattan-data/. The same views, with code and findings, are in the Milestone 3 notebook milestone3/team17-q1-q4.ipynb.
